# Compound words as gates

Status: placeholder owner idea, 2026-11-15. Needs an interview.

## Idea
Test compound vocabulary, not just single kanji: e.g. 家具 → かぐ or → "furniture". Today every gate tests one kanji, and vocabulary is fetched only for reading stories.

## Unlock order (added 2026-11-17)
Follow WaniKani's principle that vocabulary comes right after its kanji: a compound (家具) unlocks once all its kanji are learned, in Built-in mode too. Compounds and their readings come from JMdict (open data), not WaniKani; copying WaniKani's vocabulary lists is out of bounds (`docs/intent/kanji-runner.md`).
