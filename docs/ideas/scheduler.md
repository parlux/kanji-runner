# A scheduler that makes things stick

Status: in progress, 2026-11-28. Intent `docs/intent/scheduler.md`, spec `docs/specs/SPEC-scheduler.md`.

## Idea
Owner, 2026-11-28: "7 at this level and 6 at older levels feels like a bit too much older and not enough re-enforcement of the current learning." Measured: the weighted draw gives the current level ~0.3–0.6 of ~7 slots. After a research check the owner chose a time-based scheduler over a fixed split.
