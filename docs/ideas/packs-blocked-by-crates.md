# Enemy packs can't run through crates

Status: raw owner idea, 2026-11-15 (during the straight-fire round). Not yet interviewed or specced.

## Idea
Enemy packs (squads) shouldn't run through crates. Today a charging pack's column passes through an unbroken crate on its way to the army.

## Open questions (for the interview)
- What does a pack do at a crate: stop behind it, flow around it, or break it?
- Does a crate then shield the army from the lanes behind it (cover)? `docs/ideas/done/crates.md` turned down "blocks as cover from packs" as too much to read at a glance.
- Balance: crates that hold packs back change how often packs arrive.

Related: `docs/ideas/straight-fire.md` (crates block the army's fire).
