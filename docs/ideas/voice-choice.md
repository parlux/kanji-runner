# Choose the gate voice

Status: raw owner idea, 2026-11-28. Not yet interviewed or specced. Follows the [sound effects](sound-effects.md) round, which ships one character voice (pre-generated files, phone voice as fallback).

## Idea
A setting to choose which voice says the word readings at gates: a character voice (the default from the sound effects round), a natural female voice, a natural male voice, or others. Owner: "can decide later".

## Open questions (for the interview)
- Which voices to offer, and from which source (VOICEVOX characters, Google/Azure natural voices).
- Download size: every voice is another full set of files (about 20 MB each); fetch only the chosen one.
- Does the phone-voice fallback follow the choice (pick a female or male system voice)?
