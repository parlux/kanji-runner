# When on'yomi, when kun'yomi

Status: owner idea, 2026-11-20. Raw; refine later (needs an interview).

## Idea
The study card gives a story for each reading but never says when you'd use which. Teach the rule of thumb: on'yomi mostly in compounds (休日, 合計), kun'yomi when the kanji stands alone or takes okurigana (休む, 合う).

## Options so far
- A short general explainer behind the on/kun labels on the card. Cheap, written once.
- An example word at the end of each reading story (kun: 合う "to fit"; on: 合計 "total"). Richer, but touches every story in `dictionary/stories/`.

## Notes
- Ties in with the [useful Japanese epic](useful-japanese.md), which already says kun'yomi lives in standalone words and on'yomi pays off in compounds.
