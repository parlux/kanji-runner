# Idea: scenery in the sky and beside the road

Status: owner idea, 2026-11-13. Intent: `docs/intent/scenery.md`. Spec: `docs/specs/SPEC-scenery.md`, tasks: `tasks/todo-scenery.md`.

The owner: "The level looks a bit empty. Like there is nothing in the sky or on the side of the road."

Five looks were mocked up live with the game's camera and track: https://claude.ai/artifact/B5RSfFA78c1VzGGJ2LWru2 (source: `docs/ideas/scenery-mockup.html`, a single page using Three.js r149 with plain meshes, so for reference only). The owner wants all five, picked at random per run.

- **Countryside:** trees, bushes, a white fence along the kerb, hills, clouds.
- **Spring village:** cherry trees, stone lanterns, small houses, a snowy peak, falling petals.
- **Race day:** grandstands with a colourful crowd, flags on the kerb, balloons.
- **Cloud road:** no ground; the track floats on pillars over a sea of clouds, with floating islands.
- **Block city:** pastel buildings with windows, pavement, street lamps, a skyline, a blimp.
