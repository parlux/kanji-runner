# Intent: highlight the sound in reading stories

Status: confirmed by the owner, 2026-11-21, after an interview. Round: own stories (epic round 3), added during Checkpoint D. Spec: `docs/specs/SPEC-own-stories.md`, section "Sound highlight". Tasks: `tasks/todo-own-stories.md` (H1, H2). The owner waived spec approval: straight to the checkpoint.

## Statement of intent
- **Outcome:** on and kun stories lose the closing explainer sentence ("Sail, drop the l, 'say': せい."). The sound-alike chunk of each word is highlighted in place ("a **den**tist sets up…"): one highlight per word, in reading order, the closest chunk even when the spelling isn't exact (**Sa**fe for せい).
- **No kana in the story:** the card already shows the hiragana above each reading story; that is the reference.
- **Kun meaning tail:** kept short ("…a pair") only when the kun word's meaning differs from the kanji's (番 "turn", つがい "pair"); dropped otherwise.
- **Review page:** shows the reading at the top of each On and Kun column, since the story no longer spells it.
- **Scope:** rewrite every on and kun story in Kanji 5–6; `STYLE.md` and the checker so new levels follow it; the owner reviews on the page.
- **Success:** the highlighted chunk plus the card's hiragana is enough to hear the reading; the owner never misses the explainer.
- **Out of scope:** sound keywords and part names (settled), meaning stories, levels past Kanji 6.

## Decisions (and why)
| Decision | Reason |
|---|---|
| Drop the kana from the story | Owner: the card shows the hiragana; the highlight is enough. |
| Closest chunk, not exact spelling | Owner: keywords were approved by ear; don't reopen them. |
| One highlight per word, no joiner | Owner confirmed: pieces read left to right, the card gives the count. |
| Keep a kun tail only when the meaning differs | Otherwise nothing on the card says what つがい means. |
