# Intent: composite parts, the chunks you can see

Status: confirmed by the owner, 2026-11-21, after an interview (from the owner's flag on 閉: "Surely the 'one slash crowbar' can become a composite shape"). Round: own stories (epic round 3), during Checkpoint D. Spec: `docs/specs/SPEC-own-stories.md`, section "Composite parts". Tasks: `tasks/todo-own-stories.md` (C1–C3). The owner waived the spec page.

## Statement of intent
- **Outcome:** a kanji's parts are the chunks you see, one level down (閉 = gate + 才), not KRADFILE's smallest strokes (gate + one + slash + crowbar).
- **Rule:** a chunk used in 2+ kanji (all levels) becomes a named part; a chunk used once is broken down further into named parts. A chunk that is itself a kanji in our levels is a part named by that kanji's meaning, as now.
- **Data:** an open decomposition dataset (KanjiVG) replaces KRADFILE for components.
- **Names:** parts the owner approved keep their names; only new parts get names.
- **Review:** new composites go on the review page with a note and 2–3 alternatives, before any story is rewritten.
- **Stories:** Kanji 1–6 meaning stories whose parts change are rewritten (and reading stories that lean on them), judged, then the owner plays Kanji 1–6 at Checkpoint D.
- **Success:** stories name a few meaningful shapes; the "one, slash, crowbar" chains and most "part not named" warnings are gone.
- **Out of scope:** stories past Kanji 6, renaming approved parts, sound keywords, the reading-story form.

## Decisions (and why)
| Decision | Reason |
|---|---|
| One level down, single-use chunks broken further | Owner: avoids hundreds of single-use parts to name. |
| Threshold 2+ kanji across all levels | A recurring chunk pays off in later levels. |
| Review page with chips first | Owner: same as before; names are the building blocks, fixing them after stories means rewriting twice. |
| All new shapes named now (not just Kanji 1–6's) | Later levels get settled names; the owner may write all levels at once. |
| KanjiVG (CC BY-SA 3.0) | Records each kanji's real structure; same licence family as our EDRDG data. |
