# Intent: useful-words order

Status: confirmed by the owner, 2026-11-17, after an interview. Epic: `docs/ideas/useful-japanese.md` (round 2). Spec: `docs/specs/done/SPEC-useful-order.md`.

## Statement of intent
- **Outcome:** Built-in levels are reordered by usefulness. Each level picks the words that do most for travel and conversation (highest weight), then spoken frequency, then manga and easy stories, for the cost of the new kanji they need. A kanji's building blocks still come before it. Pace stays as today: 30 kanji and about 30 words per level.
- **User:** the owner, on a trip to Japan: menus, stations, signs, shops, prices, polite talk. Manga and easy readers later.
- **Why now:** today's grade order teaches near-useless characters early (干, 乙); this is the core step to dropping WaniKani by 2027-03-14.
- **Themes:** about 10 hand-written situation lists (eating, trains, shopping, hotel, directions, numbers/time/money, people, small talk), written by us. The picker is automatic; the owner doesn't review lists.
- **Progress:** learned kanji stay learned wherever they land; the player is placed at the first mostly-unlearned level.
- **Success:** mainly a coverage report: the share of a menu, station signs, a phrasebook and a free graded reader readable at levels 5/10/20, new order vs old order vs the owner's WaniKani levels (WaniKani figures scratchpad only). Plus the owner skims the first 3–4 levels for anything useless.
- **Out of scope:** the progress display (round 3), stories for the new order (round 4), WaniKani mode, changing the level size, kana-only words like すみません.

## Decisions (and why)
| Decision | Reason |
|---|---|
| Keep today's pace | Owner likes it; levels are only batches ("I'm level 11, so what?"), so no design effort on level structure. |
| Travel themes weigh most | The owner's real use in the next year is a trip to Japan. |
| Coverage report as the main check | Success is measured by what can be read, not feel; feel is a secondary skim. |
| Sample texts assembled by us | From open sources plus short menus and signs we write; the owner supplies nothing. |
| Carry progress over by kanji | No wipe; the owner already accepted progress shifting when the ordering changes. |
